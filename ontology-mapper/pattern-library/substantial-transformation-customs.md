# Substantial Transformation (Customs)

Structural source for the hardest class of limb: a vague qualitative word
("substantial", "material", "directly related") the regulator left undefined.

## Structural Match
- **Hardest-limb discriminator:** a century of practice operationalising "substantially transformed".
- **Multi-factor, not single threshold:** tariff-shift, value-added %, change in name/character/use, applied together with precedent.
- **Materiality gate:** the value-added percentage is an explicit quantitative threshold.
- **Exemption:** "simple assembly" carve-outs must be argued on the facts.
- **Compounding:** each ruling becomes precedent — a living reference base.

## Kinetic Actions
- Ambiguous limb → `run-multi-factor-discriminator`.
- Borderline value → `apply-materiality-percentage`.
- Assembly claim → `apply-exemption-check`.
- Novel facts → `escalate-to-ruling` + `writeback-precedent`.

## OKF Relationship Map
- This pattern **governs** resolution of any undefined "substantial" limb.
- The discriminator is composed of factors, each **part-of** the determination.
- A ruling is **derived-from** the scored factors and prior precedent.
- New precedent may **supersede** an earlier borderline determination.
- Resolves the hardest limb left open by [Immune T-Cell Two-Signal](immune-t-cell-two-signal.md) — **references**.
